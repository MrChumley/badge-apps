# Artillery

A turn-based two-tank artillery duel on destructible terrain, for one player against the CPU or two players sharing the badge.

## How to play

- **Menu:** Up/Down picks a row, Left/Right changes it (Mode: 2 Players or vs CPU, CPU difficulty, Lives 1/3/5), Select starts.
- **Aim:** Left/Right swings the barrel the way you press (angle), Up/Down raises or lowers power. Hold to repeat. The HUD shows L/R next to ANG and U/D next to POW.
- Menu navigation is unchanged: Up/Down picks a row, Left/Right changes its value.
- **Fire:** Select.
- Wind (yellow arrow) changes every turn. Hit the other tank to take a life; the last tank standing wins.
- **Back**, **Menu** or **Home** returns to the menu. On the winner screen, Select starts a rematch.

No Wi-Fi, sound or `secrets.py` needed. Settings are saved under the `artillery` state key.
